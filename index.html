Surgical Robotics Research

Simulation, Finite Element digital Twin Modelling, Tactile Perception and Sim-to-real transfer for Robotic Surgery. The work below builds physics-based digital twins of deformable objects and soft tissues, extracts contact forces from them, and uses those forces to drive Robotic Palpation for Tumor Localization or semi-automated motions for robotic manipulations in surgical skills tasks.

Maintained by Abhinaba Bhattacharjee, PhD candidate in Robotics and Biomechanics.

Liver Tumor Surgical Twin

Three finite element paradigms for representing a stiff tumour inside softer liver tissue, compared under gravity and under robotic palpation, with the goal of a digital twin that stays continuous and still runs at interactive frame rates.

SimVinci

A real-time surgical simulation framework compatible with the da Vinci Research Kit, built around shared motion primitives for semi-automated manipulation of both rigid and deformable objects.
